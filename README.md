### ❓Q&A

<details>
<summary><b>What is your stance on AI?</b></summary>
<br>
I like using it mostly locally because I don't want to give too much data to big AI labs. When I use it, it's strictly for proofing a concept, and then I rebuild it myself anyway. Otherwise, I mostly use it for asking questions or compacting information. I am heavily against replacing developers, as AI should be a tool rather than automation. I’m skeptical of AI-produced code security and maintainability. If someone wants to use AI, they should label it (and so will I). I tend to avoid it for learning or building final products unless it's strictly educational. People can disagree, and I love discussing it with facts I haven't heard yet, but I won't force my opinion on anyone. I just think creativity should stay human, and machines should only handle the easy, repetitive, annoying tasks.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What do you actually build?</b></summary>
<br>
Mostly things that pop into my head or serve a real purpose. If I'm building something, it should solve a problem, help a friend out, or teach me something new. It's often related to automation, improving existing software, or solving weird problems no one else thought of.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What's your deal with tech stacks and setups?</b></summary>
<br>
I'm a generalist rather than a specialist. I love tinkering and broadly applying what I know. I will stubbornly use tools even if they aren't strictly meant for the job, and I wouldn't be running Linux if I wasn't a bit of a masochist. My ideal workflow centers around the terminal and Zed, but when it comes to the stack, it entirely depends on what I'm building. Whether it's low-level systems, scripts, web apps, or hardware tinkering, I use whatever gets the job done and satisfies my curiosity.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What are your favorite things outside of code?</b></summary>
<br>
Great music, cooking and eating my own food (healthier, nicer, made myself), gaming, assembling computers, and bringing things back to life that people deemed completely broken. I'm also a part-time stage technician handling light, sound, and video recording for events, and I love traveling, nature, wolves, the moon, the color red, Paulaner Spezi, and doing stupid things with friends.
</details>
